Paste into Play Console → Main store listing → Full description.

**App name / title:** My Paisa

---

My Paisa turns your Android SMS inbox into a clear picture of your money — without a bank login and without sending your messages to a server.

HOW IT WORKS
• Grant SMS read permission once.
• My Paisa scans bank and UPI alert SMS on your phone.
• Amounts, merchants, banks, and masked account endings become transactions automatically.
• Savings accounts, credit cards, and loans are discovered and grouped for you.
• Optional: mint a simple cash transaction when something never hit SMS.

WHAT YOU GET
• Home dashboard for this month’s spend and income
• Transactions list with search, filters, and sorting
• Day Strip “Paisa Coin” day ledger
• Budgets you control
• Stats and date-range reports
• You section with detected accounts
• Transaction detail that can show the original bank alert (loaded on demand — SMS bodies are not stored)

BUILT FOR INDIA
Supports major banks and UPI wallets (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, IDFC, PNB, Federal / Fi / Jupiter, Yes Bank, IndusInd, BOB, HSBC, Slice, Paytm, PhonePe, GPay, and more). Coverage improves as templates evolve.

PRIVACY
• Processing happens on your device
• No My Paisa backend — we don’t upload your inbox
• Only financial alerts that pass the filter are saved as transactions
• Clear local data anytime in Privacy settings
• Privacy policy: https://ak0982.github.io/paisa/privacy
  (Host docs/privacy_policy.md before relying on this link.)

PERMISSIONS
My Paisa needs READ_SMS so it can find bank and UPI alerts. Personal chats are not stored as transactions. Reading the inbox is how auto-import works.

My Paisa is a personal-finance companion. It does not move money, open bank accounts, or replace your banking apps.
