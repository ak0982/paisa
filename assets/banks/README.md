# Bank logo assets

Simplified geometric marks for Indian banks shown in Paisa's account list.

## Source / licensing

These SVGs are **original simplified recreations** drawn for Paisa (personal
finance app). They are **not** official bank marketing artwork and are not
scraped high-res brand assets. Shapes and brand colors are chosen only to be
recognizable at ~40px avatar size.

Trademarks remain with the respective banks. Assets are for on-device display
of the user's own accounts in this private app.
